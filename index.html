Frequently Asked Questions

What are Band Beanz?

Band Beanz is a collection of 10,000 unique NFTs on the Ethereum blockchain that provide both digital ownership and real-world utility for music enthusiasts and concert-goers.

Why Band Beanz?

Our collection bridges the gap between digital NFT ownership and real-world music experiences, offering ticket access, exclusive events, and community benefits that grow over time.

What is the minimum viable product?

The MVP includes the NFT collection itself, the smart contract for minting and ownership tracking, a dedicated platform for redeeming concert tickets, and the community governance system.

When is the mint?

The public mint is scheduled for Q3 2023 with a mint price of 0.08 ETH. Whitelist participants will have early access 24 hours before the public mint.

How is rarity calculated?

Rarity is determined by the combination of traits each NFT possesses. Some traits are much rarer than others, and the overall rarity score is calculated based on the combined rarity of all traits.
